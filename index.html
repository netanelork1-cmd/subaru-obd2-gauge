Find a parameter

Subaru doesn't publish the request codes for AWD clutch duty or alternator load. This sweeps a range of mode-22 requests on one control unit and lists every one that answers, so you can match a reading against a value you already trust.

7E1 is the transmission computer (it also runs the AWD clutch), 7E0 the engine computer. A good first sweep is 7E1 from 1000 to 10FF — CVT temperature answers at 1017 there. Each step takes about a third of a second, so 256 steps is about a minute and a half.

Session log
